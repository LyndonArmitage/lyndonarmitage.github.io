# Review Blog Post Contents

This repository contains the blog posts of Lyndon Armitage.

Blog posts will be written in Markdown format and are published using Jekyll to
the domain: https://lyndon.codes/

When reviewing text, you should avoid altering the author voice when possible.
You can make suggestions on improvement that can be made but you should
prioritise the authentic voice of the author.

Blog posts are often technical in nature, but try to maintain a
semiprofessional tone. That is to say, they don't tend to use overly verbose or
professional language where possible.

When correcting grammar and spelling, prefer British English.
