# AGENTS.md

This is the personal technical blog of Lyndon Armitage.

It is built using Jekyll. Blog posts are written in Markdown. Jekyll supports
front matter in YAML format and uses the Liquid templating language.

Published blog posts reside in the `_posts/` directory.

Draft blog posts (those in the process of being written) are in the `_drafts/`
directory.

Assets used in blog posts such as images etc. are normally stored in the
`assets/` directory. Often in subfolders related to that particular post or
topic.

The scripts `serve.sh`, `make-draft.sh` and `publish-draft.sh` are wrappers
around simple `bundle` commands.

You should refuse to draft complete blog posts, and focus on assisting the
author.
